# Adobe Stock Credit + Subscription Pricing UI Screenshots

This directory contains visual examples of Adobe Stock's credit and subscription pricing UI, collected on April 10, 2026.

## Overview

Adobe Stock uses a dual pricing model:
1. **Subscription Plans** - Monthly/annual plans with credit stipends (10, 25, 40, 750 assets/month)
2. **Credit Packs** - One-time purchase packs (5, 16, 40, 80, 150 credits) for pay-as-you-go flexibility

## Key Patterns Observed

### Pricing Strategy
- Clear two-path approach: Subscriptions for regular users, Credit Packs for occasional users
- Subscription plans emphasize monthly cost and rollover benefits
- Credit packs emphasize bulk savings (up to US$299 saved on 150-credit pack)
- Free trial prominently featured (30 days, 10-25 assets)

### UI Design Patterns
- **Card-based selection**: Both subscriptions and credit packs use card/tile layouts
- **Highlighted recommendations**: Visual emphasis on popular/recommended options
- **Savings badges**: "Save US$X" prominently displayed on credit packs
- **Comparison layouts**: Side-by-side plan comparison for easy decision-making
- **Toggle switches**: Annual vs Monthly commitment toggle
- **Asset equivalency**: Shows what you can buy (e.g., "40 Standard Images OR 5 HD videos")
- **Rollover benefits**: Explicitly called out for subscriptions
- **Cancel anytime**: Risk-free messaging

### Integration Points
- Deep integration with Creative Cloud apps (Photoshop, etc.)
- In-app browsing and licensing without leaving design tools
- Single-sign-on across Adobe ecosystem

### Call-to-Actions
- "Start free trial" (subscriptions)
- "Buy for US$[amount]" (credit packs)
- "Continue" (general progression)
- Blue buttons (primary actions)
