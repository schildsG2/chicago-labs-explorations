G2 Activate Email Templates

Weekly prospect digest email design explorations

About This Epic

These email templates explore different visual treatments for the G2 Activate weekly digest. Each option presents the same core content (prospect count, company size breakdown, top locations, CTA) with varying emphasis on the metrics display. All variants are optimized for email client compatibility using table-based layouts and inline styles.

Option 1: Subtle Background Boxes

Light gray rounded boxes with inline numbers and labels. Most compact, subtle emphasis on metrics.
