Activate Feed Dashboard

Iterating on the scrollable feed of companies viewing your profile. Focus on optimizing the presentation of company information, intent signals, and engagement patterns.

Explorations

01 — Base Feed

Initial implementation with two-column layout: stats and CTA on left, scrollable company feed on right. Blurred company names, basic intent chips, and timestamp display.

02 — Inline Metadata

Combines related fields on single lines with bullet separators. Company context (employees, location, industry) on one line, intent + timestamp on another. ~30-40% height reduction while maintaining all information.

03 — Two-Column Layout

Grid layout within cards: company info on left, intent chip + timestamp on right. Creates strong visual hierarchy with intent signals prominently positioned top-right. ~30-40% height reduction with natural scanning pattern.

04 — Tight Spacing

Same vertical structure as base feed but with reduced padding (24px → 16px), tighter gaps (12px → 6px), and compact line-height (1.3). ~15-20% height reduction — subtle but noticeable density improvement.

05 — Inline with Pipes

Builds on inline metadata with intent badge positioned top-right and faint gray pipe separators (|) instead of bullets. Clean visual hierarchy with prominent intent signals. ~35-45% height reduction with improved scannability.

06 — Unified Container

All listings consolidated into a single white container with rounded corners and Elevate shadow-1. Rows separated by subtle dividers instead of individual cards. Maximum space efficiency with clean, unified appearance.

07a — Unified Container Refined

Refinement of the unified container approach. Starting with variant A, preparing for A/B/C testing of layout refinements and interaction patterns.
